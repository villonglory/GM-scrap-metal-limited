03 — How it works

Selling your scrap, step by step

A clear process from first contact to completed transaction. No obligation until terms are agreed.

  1. Step 01

    Tell us about your scrap

    Send the material type, an estimated quantity, its location and, if possible, photographs, using the form or WhatsApp.

  2. Step 02

    We review and respond

    Our team reviews the details, asks any questions, and confirms whether it is material we can take on.

  3. Step 03

    Agree the arrangement

    We discuss price, payment (cash or credit, where agreed) and logistics with you directly. Nothing is final until both sides agree.

  4. Step 04

    Complete the transaction

    The material is handled and the transaction completed on the terms agreed.
