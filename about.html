About us

Building dependable relationships in scrap metal trading

Contact Our Team

GM Scrapmetal Limited is a scrap metal buying and trading business based on Lindi Street, Ilala, in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. We buy scrap metal and other scrap materials, including metal scrap, obsolete materials, and industrial scrap, from individuals, suppliers, and businesses.

On the other side of the trade, we supply scrap materials to companies that process or trade scrap. Working between suppliers and industrial buyers means we understand what each side needs: clear descriptions, honest communication, and arrangements that are agreed before material moves.

Depending on the material and the transaction, we discuss cash payments and credit arrangements, and we take part in or arrange auctions where applicable. Every arrangement is agreed directly with our team.

Our focus is long-term relationships with suppliers, customers, and business partners, built one well-handled transaction at a time.
