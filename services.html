How it works

A clear process

  1. Step 01

    Tell us about your scrap

    Send the material type, an estimated quantity, its location and, if possible, photographs.

  2. Step 02

    We review and respond

    Our team reviews the details and confirms whether it is material we can take on.

  3. Step 03

    Agree the arrangement

    Price, payment and logistics are discussed with you directly. Nothing is final until both sides agree.

  4. Step 04

    Complete the transaction

    The material is handled and the transaction completed on the terms agreed.
